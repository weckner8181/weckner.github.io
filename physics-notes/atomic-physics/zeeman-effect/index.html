The Zeeman effect

Template example — introductory content retained from the original Physics notes page.

In the presence of an external magnetic field, atomic energy levels can split. To first order, the energy shift is

\[ \Delta E = \mu_B g_J m_J B. \]

Here, \(\mu_B\) denotes the Bohr magneton, \(g_J\) the Landé factor, and \(m_J\) the magnetic quantum number.
